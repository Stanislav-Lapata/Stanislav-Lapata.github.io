About Me

I'm a Senior Backend Engineer with 10+ years of commercial experience building and maintaining production systems, primarily with Elixir, Phoenix, Ruby on Rails, and PostgreSQL.

My main areas of expertise include backend architecture, distributed systems, real-time messaging, asynchronous processing, and API development. I enjoy working with concurrent systems and actor-based architectures, designing reliable services, and solving complex engineering challenges.

Throughout my career, I've designed and implemented systems involving high-volume messaging, WebSocket communication, background processing, and large-scale data operations. I'm also experienced in optimizing PostgreSQL queries and reporting workflows, working with geospatial data, and improving the performance and reliability of production applications.

Although backend engineering is my primary focus, I have also worked across the entire application stack, contributing to web, desktop, and mobile applications built with React, TypeScript, Electron, and React Native.

I value clean architecture, maintainable code, thoughtful technical decisions, and practical solutions to real-world problems.

Outside of work, I enjoy reading, cycling, and playing strategy board games.
